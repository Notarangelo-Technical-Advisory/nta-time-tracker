NTA Time Tracker Context Map

This map shows the six areas (bounded contexts) inside the NTA Time Tracker, what each one owns, and how each one connects to the others and to the outside systems it reads or writes. The admin dashboard and the hours report only read Time Tracking and Invoicing, so they are not areas of their own.

Three ways in

Admin pagesJack: time, customers, projects, invoices, status reports, users
Customer portalCustomers, by invite: their own projects, time and invoices, read-only
MAISIEJack's assistant writes unbilled time from another Firebase project

The context map

Kinds of area

  • Core. The reason the app exists: Jack's hours become invoices.
  • Supporting. Built for this app, and it serves the core.
  • Generic. Sign-in and access control that any app with customers would need.
  • Outside system. This app does not own its data or its code.

Lines and labels

  • Green line, OHS (open host service). This area reads the user's profile from Users & Invites, through the rules and the guards.
  • ACLAnti-corruption layer. Code that translates between an outside system and this app's own terms.
  • CFConformist. One side accepts the other side's model as it is.
  • C/SCustomer and supplier. One area depends on what the other area provides.

Each connection, and the code behind it

ConnectionPatternHow it works
Users & Invites → every areaOHSEvery rule in firestore.rules, and adminGuard and customerGuard, read userProfiles/{uid}. The role decides admin access. The customerId decides which records a customer can read.
Customers & Projects → Time TrackingC/SEach entry carries a customerId and a projectId, chosen from the active projects in the time entry form.
Customers & Projects → InvoicingC/SgenerateInvoice in invoice.service.ts prices each line at its project's hourly rate. The invoice PDF shows the customer's address.
Time Tracking → InvoicingC/SgenerateInvoice creates the invoice, then time-entry.service.ts marks its entries billed in one batch. Paid marks them paid. Cancel makes them unbilled again.
Time Tracking → Status ReportsC/SThe report wizard sends a period's entries, with their project names, to generateStatusReport. The saved report keeps their timeEntryIds.
Status Reports → Claude APIACLgenerateStatusReport in functions/src/index.ts turns the entries and prior outcomes into a prompt, and reads the sections back through a JSON schema. It drops sections with no activities.
Invoicing, Time Tracking, Customers & Projects → Customer PortalCFThe portal pages read the same records that the admin pages write. The rules limit them to the customer's customerId.
MAISIE → Time TrackingCFMAISIE's fta-client.ts writes entries in this app's own shape, as unbilled with source: "maisie". It uses the Admin SDK, so firestore.rules do not check these writes. It also reads customers, projects and invoices.

Three points to keep in mind

Two areas are core.

Time Tracking and Invoicing together turn Jack's hours into money. Invoicing moves an entry from unbilled to billed and paid. A change there needs the most care and the most tests.

The rules keep customers apart.

Customers sign in to the same app as Jack. firestore.rules decides what each one sees, from the customerId on their profile. Every rule change needs a test in firestore-rules.spec.ts.

MAISIE writes past the rules.

MAISIE uses the Admin SDK, so the rules do not check its entries. A change to the TimeEntry shape must keep jax-assistant's fta-client.ts working.
